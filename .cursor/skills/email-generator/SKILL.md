# Email Generator

## Quick Start
1. Clarify the request, available inputs, constraints, and success criteria.
2. Identify recipient, objective, tone, constraints, and required attachments or facts.
3. Draft with a clear ask, relevant context, and next step.
4. Return the result in the requested format with assumptions and open risks.

## Output Guidance
- Return subject line, email body, and optional shorter variant.
- Keep findings concise, traceable, and separated from speculation.
