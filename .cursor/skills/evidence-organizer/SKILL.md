# Evidence Organizer

## Quick Start
1. Clarify the request, available inputs, constraints, and success criteria.
2. Group materials by issue, claim element, source, date, and admissibility concern.
3. Track chain of custody, authenticity questions, and missing corroboration.
4. Return the result in the requested format with assumptions and open risks.

## Output Guidance
- Return an exhibit index, fact-to-evidence map, or prioritized evidence checklist.
- Keep findings concise, traceable, and separated from speculation.
