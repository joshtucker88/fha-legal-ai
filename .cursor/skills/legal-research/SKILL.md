# Legal Research

## Quick Start
1. Clarify the request, available inputs, constraints, and success criteria.
2. Identify jurisdiction, issue, posture, and controlling legal standard.
3. Gather controlling authority before persuasive authority, and flag unverifiable sources.
4. Return the result in the requested format with assumptions and open risks.

## Output Guidance
- Provide citations, rule summaries, confidence notes, and research gaps.
- Keep findings concise, traceable, and separated from speculation.
